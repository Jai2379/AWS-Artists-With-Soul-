Does this ruin the image quality?

No. The perturbation is calculated to be imperceptible to the human visual system, operating at high frequencies that blend into normal pixel variations.

Are my images sent to your server?

Yes, images are sent to our GPU servers to compute the complex PyTorch adversarial gradients. However, images are processed securely in-memory and deleted immediately.

Which models does this block?

It creates noisy gradients that disrupt standard convolutional architectures (like those used in Stable Diffusion training pipelines) and breaks feature matching.

Is it 100% foolproof?

AI models constantly evolve. This is an adversarial arms race. Our perturbations significantly degrade training value, but nothing is a perfect shield forever.
